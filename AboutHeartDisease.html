About Heart Disease

Everything you need to know about heart disease, its causes, symptoms, and prevention.

What is Heart Disease?

Heart disease, also known as cardiovascular disease, refers to conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels. It includes coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, and more. Globally, cardiovascular diseases cause over 17 million deaths annually, making them the leading cause of death worldwide.

Types of Heart Disease

Arrhythmias

Irregular heartbeats such as atrial fibrillation or tachycardia.

Coronary Artery Disease

Plaque buildup in arteries that reduces blood flow to the heart.

Heart Failure

Inability of the heart to pump enough blood for body needs.

Causes & Risk Factors

  • Poor diet (high in saturated fats and sugar)
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Smoking and excessive alcohol consumption
  • Obesity and diabetes
  • High blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Stress and family history of heart disease

Warning Signs & Symptoms

  • Chest pain or discomfort (angina)
  • Shortness of breath
  • Palpitations or irregular heartbeat
  • Extreme fatigue or dizziness
  • Swelling in the legs, ankles, or feet

Prevention Tips

Eat Healthy

Exercise Daily

Avoid Smoking

Did You Know?

Heart disease kills more people annually than all forms of cancer combined.

A healthy lifestyle can prevent over 80% of heart-related problems.
