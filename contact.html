Frequently Asked Questions

How can I contact NeoCardio?
You can reach us by phone, email, or by filling out the contact form on this page. Our team will respond as soon as possible.
What kind of heart care services do you provide?
At NeoCardio, we offer comprehensive services to prevent, diagnose, and treat heart conditions. This includes advanced diagnostics, personalized treatment plans, and ongoing support from experienced cardiac specialists.
Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment?
While some services may require a referral from your primary care physician, you are welcome to contact us directly to discuss your needs. Our team will help guide you through the process.
Where are you located?
Please check our Contact Us Page for addresses and directions to the NeoCardio center.
How soon can I get an appointment?
We aim to see new patients as quickly as possible. Contact us, and our scheduling team will do their best to accommodate you at the earliest available time.
Do you offer preventive heart health consultations?
Yes! We believe prevention is key to heart health. You can book a consultation to learn about lifestyle changes, risk assessments, and personalized preventive care.
Who are your heart specialists?
Our team consists of highly experienced, board-certified cardiac specialists who combine advanced technology with compassionate care. You can learn more about them on our Doctors page.
